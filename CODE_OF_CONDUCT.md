# Contributor Covenant — Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community
- **Correcting errors openly.** In this project, an overclaimed capability is a
  safety problem, not a style problem.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- **Publishing school network details, CCTV information, credentials, or
  student personal data**
- **Representing an unverified capability as operational**
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the **repository owner**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

The project team is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter
of any incident.

## Special considerations for this project

This is a school project involving a physical robot that will operate among
children. The following are additionally treated as serious violations:

- Claiming a capability has been tested or demonstrated when it has not
- Publishing internal school network, CCTV, or security information
- Suggesting any modification to a lift control system
- Suggesting any surveillance, identification, or tracking of students
- Encouraging unsupervised operation of the robot

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
