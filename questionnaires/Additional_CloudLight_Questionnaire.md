## START HERE

### What this document is

A structured questionnaire to establish what CloudLight **can** do, what it **may**
do, and what **remains unknown** about this school's Windows smartboards, network,
timetable, substitution process, NAS, identity systems and policies.

It exists because the architecture cannot be finalised without answers from the
school, its IT department, its timetable/administration department, its library
staff and its facilities team.

### What this document is NOT

- ❌ It is **not** an implementation plan.
- ❌ It contains **no answers**. Every `Answer` and `Evidence / Source / Attachment`
  field is deliberately empty and every `Status` is `UNANSWERED`.
- ❌ It contains **no production code**.
- ❌ It does **not** claim any capability has been tested on this school's hardware.
- ❌ No school-specific answer has been supplied or inferred anywhere in this file.

### Relationship to SHANA

CloudLight is the **platform**. SHANA is **one subsystem** of it.

> **CloudLight may submit an authorised delivery request to SHANA. CloudLight must
> never send motor, path, speed or actuator commands.** SHANA independently
> validates route safety, localisation, battery reserve, hazards and safe stopping.
> **CloudLight must continue to operate fully when SHANA is offline.**
> See `docs/cloudlight/shana_integration.md`.

### How to fill it in

1. Work through **P0** questions first. They are safety, permission, identity or
   fundamental-feasibility questions that block everything else.
2. Answer only what you can support with evidence. **"Unknown" is a valid and
   valuable answer** — it is far better than a guess.
3. Where a question says `Blocks? = YES`, leave it unanswered rather than guessing.
4. Put documents, photos, screenshots or links in `Evidence / Source / Attachment`.
5. Set `Status` to `VERIFIED` **only** when an attached piece of evidence supports
   the answer. `ANSWERED` means a person replied; `VERIFIED` means evidence exists.
6. Use `Follow-up Required` to record what must happen next.
7. **Never enter a real password, key or credential in any column.** Ask for the
   policy or a screenshot with values redacted instead.

# Q. OTA updates, integrity verification and rollback — `CL-OTA`

> **Context.** For CloudLight, "an update" is not a single thing. # R. Security, privacy and threat model — `CL-SEC`

> **Context.** CloudLight sits in rooms where the people using it are children, the hardware is shared and unattended between periods, and the network is the school's own LAN — which is not a trusted environment simply because it is internal.
